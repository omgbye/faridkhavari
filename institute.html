The Institute for a Zero-Cost Economy

A blueprint to create general economic security in a carefree economy.

The Institute for a Zero-Cost Economy promotes policies designed to create the maximum economic security for every citizen in a carefree economy. These policies create wealth for everyone by freezing, then reducing and in some cases eliminating costs which prevent people from accumulating wealth. These policies create secure jobs in America.

The Idea, In Numbers

What if costs stopped rising?

Every day, the costs a family must pay increase, and most people are no longer able to save. The Institute's founding question is what happens to a family's wealth when the biggest costs are frozen, reduced, or eliminated instead:

$300,000+

kept by a family that pays off a $200,000 mortgage in 10 years instead of 30.

$100,000+

saved when a household pays for electricity for 7 years instead of a whole lifetime.

$100,000+

more in the family's hands when insurance costs are cut in half.

$100–200k

saved by a car that runs on free electricity and lasts 15 years instead of 3 or 4.

The Presentation

His graphic presentation, restored

In 2009, Dr. Khavari animated his entire argument as a graphic presentation for the Institute's first website: incomes standing still while costs climb, the debt wedge growing between them — and then the way out. The original was built in Flash, which no browser can play anymore. It has been restored and rebuilt, faithful to his drawing. Press play.

The trap, and the way out: freeze costs, then reduce them, then eliminate them — and the wedge between income and costs becomes savings and wealth.

The Program

The goals

The Institute's goals, beginning in Florida — the pilot state for a carefree economy:

Employment

Full employment within a year, achieved mainly through decentralized energy systems, vehicle conversion, and productivity-inducing healthcare clinics — creating the conditions to retire after 50 and move toward a leisure- and knowledge-based society.

Energy

Decentralized solar, wind, and passive systems, promoted and manufactured at scale, until energy is free for Florida homes, buildings, factories, and vehicles — Florida as a solar wonderland, leading the world in research and manufacturing.

Home ownership

Home ownership possible for every Florida family within 15 years at a maximum of 3% interest — and within 10 years at zero interest, with true ownership once the mortgage is paid.

Insurance

One universal, affordable policy covering health care, car, life, natural disaster, theft, and flood — high-quality coverage every Floridian can actually pay for.

Business

Every business owning its own building through zero-interest financing, and long-term tax exemptions for the projects that serve the general economic and environmental interests of the state and the nation.

Education

Education free, including graduate and post-graduate study — no student or family buried in debt for a degree.
